## 1. Rules for the real device

- The CPU draws the UI: solid surfaces, no blur, no shadows. Alpha only for rounded corners and the faded side items of a carousel / wheel.
- Fonts: Inter (Latin, digits) + IBM Plex Sans Thai (Thai), OFL. Line height 1.25.
- Icons: stroke line icons, stroke 2 on a 24 grid, one set (the SVG paths in `mockup_v2.py` `ICONS`).
- Animation: one tween, ease-out 180 ms, on pre-scaled images (scale covers/logos once when loaded, never per frame). Target ≥ 58 fps (NX_BENCH).
- Every token lives in one file, `res/bos/tokens.h` (C and C++). No hex colour or font size outside it.
- **Draw order (whole OS, YouTube too), back to front:**
  1. page background;
  2. the picture or card (its own rounded corners are part of it, made once when it is loaded);
  3. what sits on the picture: watched bar, duration pill, badges;
  4. the focus ring, **last**;
  5. anything that must cover the ring on purpose (a check badge on a corner), then overlays (toast, sheet, popup).
  - Never "erase" with the background colour: no bg-coloured shape painted over another to cut a hole or to round a corner on screen. An outline is drawn as one ring shape (`BOS_ring` / `BOS_focusRing`, YTC `Renderer::ring`).
  - Corner masks may only touch the inside of their own rectangle. They still must come before the ring, because a rectangle's square corner reaches into the ring's curve.
  - A page that redraws only what moved must start whole after anything else touched the screen (sleep, a popup, another app).

- **Focus** (made stronger 2026-11-10): fill `focus`, text/icon `onfocus`, weight 600.
  - Scale **1.12** for tiles, cards and quick-settings tiles. Full-width rows and sliders scale **1.04**, since they cannot grow more.
  - **Everything else that can be focused on the screen is drawn at 60 % alpha:** other tiles, rows, cards and buttons. Titles, captions, the status bar and the hint bar never dim.
  - Labels under tiles are `text` (white) at rest, so at 60 % they stay ≥ 7:1.
  - Cost: the dim is a per-texture alpha mod, and the focused tile is pre-rendered at 1.12. Nothing is drawn per frame.
- **Selected but not focused** (the open Settings category): fill `surface2`, text `text`, full alpha.
- **Dark only.** There is no light mode in UI 2.0 (decided 2026-11-10); it may come later. ธีมสี › พื้นหลัง still offers ดำ / เทาเข้ม.
- **Picture focus:** ring 4 px `focus`, offset 4. Pictures never get the white fill.

### 5.7 Motion
- Every animation is ease-out and moves **pictures already drawn**.
- The leaving screen and the coming screen are each rendered once to a texture. After that, only their positions and alpha change per frame.
- No blur, no shadows. Target ≥ 58 fps, measured with NX_BENCH.

| What | How | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Push a page / back | The new page slides in from the right. The old one moves left 30 % and darkens to 35 %. Back plays it reversed. | 240 ms |
| Open an app or game | The focused tile scales up to full screen, then fades to black. The app starts. | 260 ms |
| Back to Home | Fade in from black | 200 ms |
| Focus, carousel, wheel | The highlight or slots tween to their new place | 120–180 ms |
| Sheet / pop-up | Slides up from the bottom; the page behind dims to 60 % black | 220 ms |
| Toggle | The knob slides; the track colour cross-fades | 150 ms |
| End of a list | A held direction with nowhere to go: the page nudges 12 px towards the wall and back, once per hold (§5.10 bump) | 160 ms |

- **As built (2026-11-13; board `design/v2-MotionSound.html`, kit `common/ui/bos_motion.c`):**
  - One curve everywhere: out-quart (the board's `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`).
  - A move starts from where the thing is: a new press mid-way bends the path, nothing jumps.
  - Wait for the picture: when the coming page still loads its art, the old page stays up for at most 150 ms, then the move starts with what is there.
  - A page that is not moving draws nothing (0 % CPU measured on Home, the wheel and the lists).
  - **60 fps budget (measured 2026-11-13, average time between frames 16.0–16.9 ms on Home, the wheel, covers, lists and Settings):**
    - Pictures, text and icons are drawn by `BOS_blit`. SDL's blit with an alpha or colour mod on a picture that has its own alpha is its slowest path (a dimmed row cost 0.4 ms, a cover 2 ms).
    - While only one part of a page moves, only that part is drawn (`BOS_partialBegin` / `BOS_partialBeginR`, page clips through `BOS_clip`): Home's Continue row, the wheel, the covers down to the hint bar, the list rows, one column of Settings. The frame after the last such frame is a whole one.
    - A page change moves two GPU pictures of the old and the new page; the page itself is drawn once.
    - Sending the changed rows to the GPU is 3 ms (a few rows) to 10 ms (the whole screen) and does not get faster with a faster clock. A press that changes the whole page is therefore one frame of about 20 ms.
    - The menu clock is held at 1416 MHz while a page moves (the governor never climbed in time for 10 ms frames); idle is 408–600.
- **ตั้งค่า › หน้าตา › ภาพเคลื่อนไหว:** เต็ม (default) / น้อย (fades only, 120 ms) / ปิด.
- This replaces v1's "Show menu animations" and "Show menu transitions".

- **Every list wraps (2026-11-16, the user's rule: "ตามหลักสากล").** In every app (OS, YouTube, the keyboards):
  a fresh press past an end goes to the other end (Down on the last row → the first row, Right on the last item →
  the first); a held direction stops at the end (the bump above). Grids read row by row: Left/Right go along the
  reading order (past a row's end to the next row's start), Up/Down by a row keeping the column (a short last row:
  its last tile). Two-row pages (Home: เล่นต่อ / apps; Video: cards / tiles): Up/Down past either end is the other
  row. Kit: `PAD_wrapStep` / `PAD_wrapNav` / `PAD_gridNav` (api.h); YTC `wrap_step` / `grid_step`.

### 5.9 System overlay: level HUD, quick panel, toasts (2026-11-13)
TrimUI's overlay daemon (`trimui_osdd`) draws over every app, games and YouTube included. Brick OS keeps the daemon and replaces everything it shows: no stock picture is left. All pictures come from `scripts/osd/make_osd.py` (tokens of §2, Inter), into `skeleton/SYSTEM/osd`. They are used as drawn: the launcher no longer tints them with the accent.

- **Level HUD (volume, brightness = SELECT + volume, colour temperature = START + volume):**
  - A 440×76 pill, centred, 12 px under the status bar (x 292, y 76). `surface`, 1 px `surface2` edge, fully round.
  - Left: the icon, 38 px (`sound`; `mute` in `text2` at 0; `sun`; `moon`). Middle: a 10 px bar, track `#3A3A3E`, fill `focus`. Right: the number, 30/600, right-aligned.
  - Up for 1.2 s after the last press. A new level replaces the picture that is up; the three kinds share one place.
  - How: one picture per level (`osd/common/hud/vol_00–20`, `bri_00–10`, `ct_00–40`). `keymon` shows it as a toast whose background is that picture. So it is the same over the menus, a game and YouTube, and the apps draw **no** indicator of their own (the old NextUI pill is off whenever the HUD pictures are on the card).
  - As built (2026-11-14): the daemon puts a step on screen 13-49 ms after the key (`.dev/OSD.md`, HUD patch); before
    the patch one step in ~12 hung for ~900 ms while it refreshed the quick panel's widgets.
- **Quick panel (HOME key):** layout unchanged (`osdlayout.json`), new skin.
  - Every level bar shows its number (2026-11-14): the brightness tile has the level right-aligned after the bar (30/600,
    `text`), as the HUD pill does; the bar keeps its length whatever the number. Settings' sliders the same: the value
    (`small`/600, `text2`, `onfocus` on the focused row) after the bar, in the room of the widest value.
  - Scrim: black 85 %. Tiles: `surface`, radius 28. Focus: a 4 px `focus` ring outside the tile (§2 focus for pictures).
  - Switches: an 80 px disc. Off = `surface2` with a `text2` glyph. On = `focus` with a dark glyph. Recording and power-off use `danger`.
  - Read-outs (CPU, RAM, TEMP): a `surface2` chip with the word in 26/700 `text2`.
  - Slider: 14 px track `#3A3A3E`, fill `focus`.
- **Toasts:** a `surface` pill 72 tall, radius 24, 1 px `surface2` edge; text 26. Centred, y 608 (above the hint bar). Icons 40 px: a round badge in `ok` / `danger` / `warn` / `primary` with a dark mark.
- Not ours yet: the daemon's own text font, and the toast wording (English).

### 5.10 Menu sounds (2026-11-13; board `design/v2-MotionSound.html`, kit `common/ui/bos_sfx.c`)
- Eight sounds: move, ok (A), back (B), bump (end of a list), on, off (switches), open (a sheet), launch (a game or app).
- Short tones made in code, no files. Three sets, **ตั้งค่า › เสียง › ชุดเสียง:** Soft (default), Click, Retro. **เสียงเมนู:** ปิด / เบา / ปกติ.
- They follow the system volume and mute, and mix over background music.
- A held direction ticks once per step; at the wall it knocks once, then stays silent.
- A `.wav` named after a sound (`move.wav` …) in `.userdata/shared/sounds/` replaces it in every set.
- Never late: a sound that could not start within 250 ms is dropped.

### 5.8 Growth rules (anything the user adds must fit without a redesign)
- **Apps:** every `Tools/*.pak`, shipped or installed (e.g. PortMaster), appears in แอปทั้งหมด by itself. An app with no icon gets the generic `box` icon in `secondary`.
- **Consoles:** a console with at least one game appears in the wheel by itself. The wheel wraps.
- **Names:** one line, cut with "…". If the focused name is cut, it scrolls slowly after 1 s.
- **Counts:** real games only (§6.2).
- **Pictures:** each picture has a fallback chain (§6.2, §5.3), so nothing is ever blank.

**Behaviour**
- Hold time is 0.5 s.
  - กดคลิก fires on release under 0.5 s (HOME already had to act on release, see keymon).
  - With a กดค้าง action, it fires at 0.5 s while the key is still down; the release after it does nothing.
  - With no กดค้าง action, a long press counts as a กดคลิก.
- HOME, F1 and F2 work in the launcher, in games and in Settings.
  - On the Brick Pro, F1/F2 are L4/R4 (evdev KEY_F1/KEY_F2). Games use L3/R3, which have their own buttons, so there is no clash.
  - From a game, an app, game, console, home or switcher action first saves the game (as สลับเกม does) and quits it.
  - From Settings, those actions close Settings first.
  - ตั้งค่าด่วน and ปิด/เปิดเสียง work in every app (keymon does them itself).
  - Other apps (YouTube, music, video, files, PortMaster) do not hand over yet: there the other actions do nothing.
- MENU keeps its in-game role: tap opens the in-game menu, hold opens สลับเกม.
- If a target is gone (app removed, ROM deleted), the press does nothing.

**Defaults**
- HOME: กดคลิก ตั้งค่าด่วน, กดค้าง สลับเกม.
- F1/F2: กดคลิก is the current fn1tool/fn2tool app (migrated), and กดค้าง is ว่าง.

**Storage** (`.userdata/shared/bos_keys.txt`, `key=value` lines)
- One key per slot: `btnhome`, `btnhomehold`, `btnf1`, `btnf1hold`, `btnf2`, `btnf2hold`; each with a `<key>.label`
  line, the name shown in Settings (taken when the value was picked).
- Values:
  - `sys:quick`, `sys:switcher`, `sys:home`, `sys:mute`
  - `app:<pak folder name>` (or `app:games`, `app:ports`)
  - `console:<Roms dir path>`
  - `game:<rom path>`
  - empty for ว่าง
- A missing key means its default. `fn1tool` and `fn2tool` are migrated once to `btnf1` and `btnf2` as `app:`.

**Who runs it**
- keymon watches HOME, F1 and F2 everywhere and times tap/hold.
  - `sys:quick` and `sys:mute` it does itself.
  - Anything else it writes to `/tmp/bos_action`, only while the launcher, a game or Settings is in front.
- The launcher runs `/tmp/bos_action` from its main loop (and at start).
- minarch sees it, saves and quits (leaving the file); the launcher then runs it.
- Settings sees it and closes (leaving the file).

### 7.4 Built (2026-11-15): where the code differs from the boards, and why
- **The shared pieces follow one switch** (`UI_bos`, ui_draw.h): menu bar → status bar, hint pairs → the hint bar (A right),
  confirm box → §7.1 card (trash/warning sign from its words), MENU popup → a bottom sheet of tiles (two columns, an icon
  per known action), toast → a pill above the hint bar, empty page, loading, message, splash, controls help, the
  settings page (Settings' rows, ‹ value › on focus) and the list dialog → a page with a group. Lists (ListView) draw as
  §5.6 groups (`bos_list.c`, rows with picture / squircle / letters, sub line, value, pill, tick, progress). Music and Video
  set it. **Next sweep:** the other apps that use these pieces (Extras, Scraper, Image Viewer, RA tools, Netplay setup,
  Cheat database, Emulator options, Game time, Sync) only need the switch and a check of their own drawing.
- **Music:** the "new playlist" row is the last row (not the first) so the selection keeps meaning the playlist.
  The file rows show the folder's picture and the format (each song's own art, artist and length need its tags read:
  later). Songs from the own server and free music are downloaded first, then played (the music service plays files
  and live radio; podcasts work the same way); they stay under Music/ดาวน์โหลด. Jamendo stays hidden until a developer
  key is set (none yet); free music searches Internet Archive's netlabels. The player sheet has no header (the shared
  sheet has none).
- **Video:** a video's picture is a same-name .jpg/.png beside it; taking a frame from the video needs an extractor the
  system does not ship yet. The player's status line has the time and battery (no WiFi icon). Its overlay is drawn by
  our ffplay with SDL_ttf and the kit's Thai glyph mapping (`include/ffplay/bos_osd.inc`), the language from BOS_LANG.
- **Still old elsewhere:** YouTube and the keyboard (round 2, next); DraStic's own menu (a closed emulator: its menu
  cannot be redrawn, only replaced by ours if DraStic's hooks allow).
